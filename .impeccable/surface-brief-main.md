# Surface brief — main (single-page app)

Scope: the whole perfume finder as one descending page. Visitor mode: Experience (the artifact leads).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Choosing a scent is a dive. Six answers take you from the bright surface to the abyss where your bottle glows; the category-default arrangement refused is a light dashboard quiz with a results card.

OWN-WORLD: Abyssal blue-black depth strata (#0B1220 family, depth increases with each step), no flat black; the only saturated light is bioluminescent juice color — each fragrance family is a glowing specimen hue; a warm lamp-gold is reserved for the focused bottle. Hairline Italiana specimen labels over a quiet Hanken Grotesk for data. Depth-gradient backgrounds, drifting mist particles, glowing pedestals.

STORY: I am descending toward my scent; the room darkens as my answers narrow the shelf; the winner surfaces as light in the dark. I can drag the bottle, read its notes like a specimen plate, and see why it won.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Bright surface band (turquoise-to-deep gradient water), giant Italiana headline "find your scent" scale ~9rem across the left, a live 3D bottle already glowing on a plinth right-of-center, one primary action "Start the dive" bottom-left, live shelf ribbon peeking at the fold. Seed key: 83002aad.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the descent — each answered step deepens the page's water gradient and dims the light, ending in the reveal: abyss, drifting motes, the winning bottle lit lamp-gold in its juice color while the match count rises.

FORM: assigned direction (candidate 6, deep-dive), challengers declined with donations: raku donates total palette commitment (one hot accent only), Riley gallery donates type held clear of the glowing object, VU bridge donates ballistic easing on match bars.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
