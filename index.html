How it works

Six questions, or words

Every step is multiple choice with a free-text alternative. What you pick in the buttons always wins; typed words only fill the gaps, so the engine can't misread you.

Scored where you sit

Answers become a weighted profile across family, season, occasion, presence and budget. Forty fragrances are scored against it in your browser, on every keystroke. Anything over your stated budget sinks in the ranking, no matter how well it scores.

Bottles built, not photographed

Each result gets a procedurally generated 3D bottle echoing its real design: Le Male's torso, Angel's star, 1 Million's gold ingot, Sauvage's gradient column. Glass, juice and light, no product photos.

The library holds 40 well-known fragrances, summarized from public note pyramids. Price bands are rough, not quotes, and there are no affiliate links. Made by Ahmed Metered.
