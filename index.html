How it works

Six questions, or words

Every step is multiple choice with a free-text alternative. Typing "cozy vanilla for winter nights" feeds the same profile the buttons do.

Scored where you sit

Answers become a weighted profile: family, season, occasion, presence, budget. Forty fragrances are scored against it in your browser, on every keystroke.

Bottles built, not photographed

Each result gets a procedurally generated 3D bottle: lathe-turned glass, juice colored by scent family, a label drawn at runtime. No product photos, no brands copied.

The library holds 40 well-known fragrances, summarized from public note pyramids. Price tiers are rough bands, not quotes, and there are no affiliate links. Made by Ahmed Metered.
